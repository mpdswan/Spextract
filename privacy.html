Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

🔒

No data collected CONFIRMED

Spextract does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. Everything happens on your device. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Spextract ("the app," "we," "our") handles information when you use the Spextract application on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. The short version: we collect nothing. The longer version follows.

2. Information we collect

We collect no information whatsoever.

Spextract does not collect, access, process, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, Apple ID, device ID, IP address)
  • Usage data (which features you use, how often you open the app, session length)
  • Diagnostic or crash data
  • Location data
  • Photos, images, or spectrum files you import into the app
  • Exported data files you generate
  • Any other information of any kind

3. How the app processes your data

All computation — image loading, perspective correction, axis calibration, trace extraction, and CSV export — runs entirely on your device using the device's own processor and memory. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Nothing leaves your device.

Images you import and data you export remain under your control at all times, stored only in your device's local file system or wherever you choose to save them (e.g. iCloud Drive, a local folder). Spextract does not retain copies of any files.

4. Third-party SDKs and services

Spextract contains no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, crash reporters, or tracking libraries of any kind.

The app does not integrate with:

  • Firebase, Crashlytics, or any Google analytics service
  • Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment, or similar product analytics tools
  • Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or advertising attribution tools
  • Any cloud storage or processing service

The only external connection the app makes is the standard App Store receipt validation handled by Apple, which is outside our control and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's privacy

Because we collect no data from anyone, Spextract is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area) — or from anyone else.

6. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect data. There is nothing to share, sell, or disclose.

7. Data retention

We retain no data. Any files or session state are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you remove them or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, and portability of your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, these rights do not require any action on our part. If you believe this is incorrect or wish to enquire, contact us at Spextract@outlook.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. As the app does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We recommend checking this page periodically if you wish to stay informed.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy? Contact us at:

Spextract@outlook.com
